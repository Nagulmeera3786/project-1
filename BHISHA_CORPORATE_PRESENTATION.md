## Design Guide for PowerPoint/Google Slides
Use this guide while converting the deck into visual slides:

- Theme: dark, premium, enterprise style
- Primary colors: charcoal/black background, white text, accent magenta/pink
- Visual asset: use the provided SVG as the brand mark (header or title slides)
- Typography: clean sans-serif with high contrast
- Layout: generous whitespace, strong section headers, minimal text density
- Charts/icons: use simple line icons for channels, security, and global expansion

Recommended slide ratio: 16:9
